Privacy Policy

Oromo Conversation Academy
Effective date: November 1, 2026

Oromo Conversation Academy is an educational app designed to help children and adults learn the Oromo language. This policy explains the data practices of the current app version, which provides offline lessons and prerecorded audio.

1. Information We Collect

The current app is designed to provide its lessons and prerecorded audio without requiring users to create an account or submit personal information. It does not include an active microphone or voice-recording feature.

We do not intentionally request names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photographs, or voice recordings through the current lessons-and-audio experience. Device-level services or third-party software included in the app may have separate data practices; any such practices must be accurately reflected here before publication.

2. How the App Works

Lessons and prerecorded audio are available offline. The current version does not display advertisements and does not offer an active in-app subscription or payment feature.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not intentionally sell or share personal information collected through the lessons-and-audio features. We will update this policy if we introduce features or third-party services that collect or share user information.

4. Children's Privacy

Oromo Conversation Academy is intended for learners of different ages, including children. The current lessons-and-audio features do not require children to provide personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions or concerns. We aim to comply with applicable children's privacy requirements, including Google Play Families policies.

5. Data Security and Retention

The current lessons-and-audio experience does not require an account or a user-submitted profile. If future versions add features that store or transmit personal information, we will describe how that information is protected, retained, and deleted.

6. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on Android system components to function. Before this policy is finalized, the developer will review the release build and any included third-party libraries to confirm whether they collect, transmit, or share data. Any identified services and practices will be disclosed here as required.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
